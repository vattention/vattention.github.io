Product Marketer — Video Creation

The Role

We are looking for a Product Marketer for video creation workflows: someone who understands the product and its users, connects insight, positioning, content, channel execution, and analysis, and takes responsibility for growth outcomes.

You need to decide what to do and execute it yourself, from a clear product proposition to a launch, a content series, or a measurable growth experiment.

Responsibilities

  • Understand target users and their workflows; define product positioning, core value, and messaging.
  • Plan and execute launches, content, landing pages, and channel experiments yourself, communicating product value to the right people.
  • Choose effective channels across content, social, search, creator partnerships, or communities, and keep testing acquisition and conversion.
  • Track key metrics from reach to signup, activation, retention, and payment; analyze experiments and adjust effort accordingly.
  • Bring user feedback and growth data into product decisions, improving positioning, product experience, and launch strategy together.

What We Look For

  • Able to own the full growth loop from user insight and positioning to content, channel execution, and analysis. Share cases you personally led and their results.
  • Strong copywriting, content, and visual judgment, with the ability to articulate product value and create marketing assets yourself.
  • Experienced in at least one growth channel and able to choose methods, design experiments, and evaluate results for the target audience.
  • Able to distinguish exposure from meaningful growth and explain metric definitions, personal contributions, and key tradeoffs.
  • Able to communicate and create content in English; proficient with AI tools for research, creation, analysis, or automation.
  • Self-directed and hands-on, willing to use the product deeply, engage with users, and take responsibility for growth outcomes.

Preferred Experience

The following experiences are preferred, not required. We place more weight on your work, judgment, and delivered results.

  • Hands-on video creation experience, with familiarity across ideation, scripting, filming, editing, and publishing.
  • An understanding of video creators’ needs, tools, and creative taste. We welcome links to your work.

About Vattention

Vattention builds AI products for human-AI collaboration. We value product taste, judgment, and hands-on delivery, with a small team that communicates directly, tests ideas quickly, and iterates continuously.

What to Include

Include growth cases, content, or launches you personally delivered, explaining the target audience, your contribution, metric definitions, results, and lessons learned.
