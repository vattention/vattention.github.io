Product Builder — Video Creation

The Role

We are looking for a Product Builder who turns ideas into shipped products for video creation workflows, independently owning user problems, ideation, technical architecture, development, launch, and iteration.

This is how Vattention’s CEO currently works: personally developing product ideas, designing technical architecture, building with AI, and taking products through launch. We expect you to take the same scope of ownership.

Responsibilities

  • Start with user problems: develop product ideas, validate demand and key assumptions, and define priorities and success criteria.
  • Own interaction prototypes, technical architecture, data models, and technology choices, balancing experience, performance, and delivery speed.
  • Use AI coding agents to build frontend and backend features and integrate services; understand, review, and maintain the code you ship.
  • Own testing, deployment, monitoring, and troubleshooting, including store submission or public launch so real users can use the product.
  • Gather user feedback after launch, use usage data to improve the product, and take responsibility for the complete experience and delivery.

What We Look For

  • Able to independently take a product through ideation, technical architecture, development, validation, and launch. Show a shipped product and explain what you personally delivered.
  • Sound engineering judgment across frontend, backend, data, third-party services, and deployment, with the ability to diagnose and fix issues in AI-generated code.
  • Proficient with AI coding tools such as Codex, Claude Code, and Cursor, using them in everyday delivery and verifying their output.
  • Product taste and empathy for users, with the ability to turn ambiguous needs into clear, usable experiences.
  • Self-directed and hands-on, able to make decisions, communicate tradeoffs, and take responsibility for outcomes.

Preferred Experience

The following experiences are preferred, not required. We place more weight on your work, judgment, and delivered results.

  • Hands-on video creation experience, with familiarity across ideation, scripting, filming, editing, and publishing.
  • An understanding of video creators’ needs, tools, and creative taste. We welcome links to your work.

About Vattention

Vattention builds AI products for human-AI collaboration. We value product taste, judgment, and hands-on delivery, with a small team that communicates directly, tests ideas quickly, and iterates continuously.

What to Include

Include links to shipped products or store listings, and explain your own contribution, key architecture decisions, and the path from idea to launch.

Use the role title as your email subject and include a resume or a brief introduction.
